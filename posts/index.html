QuipuNet: single-molecule analysis using deep-learning

2018-06-07 We trained a convolutional neural network to read nanopore signals. It classifies 95% of events where the previous method managed 18%, with no expert checking, at 1600 events per second.

PhD thesis

2016-05-29 Hydrodynamic interactions between particles decay as 1/r in bulk. Confine those particles to a narrow channel and they stop decaying at all. That surprise is the core of my PhD thesis.

Non-decaying particle interactions

2015-09-19 We found a particle-particle interaction that does not weaken with distance. Confine two particles in a narrow, water-filled channel and they keep feeling each other however far apart they drift.
